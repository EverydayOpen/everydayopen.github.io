How we build

Small apps with a few firm rules.

  1. Free. Really.

    No trial, no paywall, no account, no upsell. The apps are free, and they stay free.

  2. Open source

    Every line is on GitHub. Read it, build it yourself and check exactly what it does.

  3. Private by default

    No analytics, no tracking, nothing sold. Your files and findings stay on your Mac.

  4. Look before touching

    Explain first, in plain English. Change as little as possible, and back up before anything does.
